Average speed camera sections

📋 About the project

This interactive map shows certified average speed camera sections on Bulgaria’s road network. The project visualizes the monitored road points where average speed is calculated and enforced.

🗺️ Open the map

🛣️ Road types

  • Motorways: shown in dark blue
  • First-class roads: shown in blue

📊 Data sources

Information for each section comes from BG-Toll. Camera coordinates are based on that page and on the Google Maps layer also published by BG-Toll.

BG-Toll · Google Maps

✨ Features

  • Turn individual road sections on or off
  • Detailed popups with information about each road section
  • Information about where the toll cameras are located

📝 Note

This project is for informational purposes. Please follow speed limits everywhere, not only in marked sections. The data shows the Bulgarian road sections current as of 24.04.2026 that are monitored for average speed violations.
